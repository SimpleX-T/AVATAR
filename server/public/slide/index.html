Statement of the problem

Many small farms still depend on the farmer being physically present.

  • Farm conditions may change between visits.
  • Water may be applied without current soil information.
  • Fire or unusual movement may be noticed too late.
  • Separate devices need separate power supplies and controls.
  • Grid electricity and internet access may be unreliable.

The problem is the absence of one small, off-grid system that can monitor the farm and coordinate these responses.

Our proposed system

Project AVATAR combines farm monitoring, routine control and protection in one ESP32-based prototype.

It monitors

  • Soil pH, EC and NPK
  • Soil moisture; air temperature and humidity
  • Light, gas, movement and fire state
  • Data freshness and pump activity
  • Future remote child-node readings

It controls

  • One bounded irrigation-pump response
  • Servo-assisted fire-pump response
  • Safe local control without internet
  • ThingSpeak data history
  • Farmer dashboard and status
4 / 10

System overview and scope

1Sensors measure the farm.
2The ESP32 checks and stores readings.
3Bounded local protection responds.
4ThingSpeak and dashboard show history.

Scope limitation

This is a controlled 2 x 3 ft wired prototype. It demonstrates one irrigation area and one fire response; it does not claim multi-zone irrigation, commercial-farm deployment, crop-yield measurement, or professional fire suppression. Future child nodes will communicate wirelessly after the wired head-node is proven.
